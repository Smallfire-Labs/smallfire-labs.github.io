A season snow log for each of your sites

For snow-removal contractors who bill by the inch: one printable season record per site. It starts with the station your contract names, adds nearby readings with miles, and lists each storm with the reading from every source. Free for one site: reply to support@smallfirelab.com with the address and, if your contract names one, the station.

Sample: a public building in Buffalo, NY, season 2025-11-01 to 2026-04-30. A free log for your site has the same weather sections in a PDF and a CSV, for last season and this season so far. It has no service entries, because we don't have yours.

What's real in the sample and what isn't

The weather readings are real. The site is a stand-in: a real public address (Buffalo City Hall) with a made-up contract. The service entries are made up to show the format, and every made-up entry is marked SAMPLE. The log is compiled automatically from public National Weather Service and CoCoRaHS reports, each named by its station ID. It is not certified by a meteorologist or by NOAA. It is meant as a backup for billing records, not for legal or insurance claims.

Beside it, the sample lists made-up service entries in the format a contractor would type them: plow, Mar 16, 04:45 to 06:15; salt, 600 lb rock salt, Mar 16, 06:15 to 06:50; shovel, Mar 17, 07:00 to 07:40. We do not check service entries.

Sources disagree, which is why the log shows all of them with distances. In storm 14 (January 25 to February 1, 2026) the airport reported 21 inches and the nearby volunteers 7.3 to 19.8. No reading is taken at the site itself, and lake-effect snow can differ a lot over a few miles. "None" means the station reported nothing for that storm, which is not the same as zero.
