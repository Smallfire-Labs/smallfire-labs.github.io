What we're doing now

Accessible copies of public meeting agendas

We rebuild a town's posted agenda as a tagged PDF. This month we're starting to email small-town clerks a free sample of their own agenda, with a short report on how it was checked. The copy is machine-checked against PDF/UA-1 and reviewed by an AI. It is not certified and was not tested with a screen reader. The town's original stays the official version.

If a free copy of your next agenda would help, write to support@smallfirelab.com. It needs to be a text PDF, not a scan.

Season snow logs for snow-removal contractors

One printable record per site, built from public National Weather Service and CoCoRaHS readings: the station your contract names, nearby readings with miles, and every storm. It is a backup for billing records, not certified and not for legal or insurance claims. Free for one site.
