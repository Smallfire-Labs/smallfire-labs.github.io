California (SB 54)

  • Producers of single-use packaging and plastic single-use food serviceware [secondary].
  • Exemption: gross annual sales under $1M, but you must register in PEPRS and apply to claim it; must plan for all packaging recyclable or compostable by 2032. Status: verified 2026-10-27 (CalRecycle exemptions page).
  • PRO: CAA, approved as first PRO [official]. CAA submitted its program plan on 2026-10-13; CalRecycle has 75 days to review; comments due 2026-11-13 to packaging@calrecycle.ca.gov, subject "PRO Plan Comments" [official]. Advisory Board meets 2026-11-06 [official].
  • Registration closed 2026-06-01, 2025-data reports due 2026-05-31, first fund payment 2027-03-01, program start by 2027-01-01: all (unverified), not on CalRecycle pages read. A secondary source gave an Aug 14 comment date; the official date is Nov 13.
  • Source: https://calrecycle.ca.gov/packaging/packaging-epr/

Oregon

  • Producers include brand owners, importers, first distributors [secondary].
  • Small producer (ORS 459A.863(32)): nonprofit, public body, gross revenue under $5M, under 1 metric ton sold in/into Oregon, beverage maker under 5 metric tons, and others. Status: verified 2026-10-27.
  • PRO annual report due July 1 (ORS 459A.887). Status: corrected 2026-10-27 (draft said May 31; producer-level May 31 date unverified).
  • PRO: CAA, approved 2025-02-21. Status: unverified.
  • Source: https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors459A.html

Colorado

  • Producers of packaging, paper products, food serviceware [secondary].
  • Exemption (C.R.S. 25-17-713, HB22-1355 as enacted): under $5M realized gross revenue (excluding on-premises alcohol) or under 1 ton of covered materials; also governments, nonprofits, small ag employers, licensed retail food establishments, builders. The Commission adjusts the $5M by rule each July 1 (Denver CPI); the current adjusted figure is unverified, as is any later amendment. Status: corrected 2026-10-27 (draft said CDPHE rule sets the de minimis level).
  • Producers may not sell covered products unless in the program from 2025-07-01. Status: verified 2026-10-27.
  • Dues: date set by the PRO, no later than 2026-01-01, then annually. Status: corrected 2026-10-27.
  • PRO: CAA since 2023-05-01 (unverified); registration closed 2024-10-01 (unverified); supply data due 2025-07-31 (unverified).
  • Source: https://leg.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/2022a_1355_signed.pdf (cdphe.colorado.gov returned 403, not used)

Minnesota

  • Exemption: under 1 ton or under $2M global gross revenue. Status: verified 2026-10-27.
  • Producers must be PRO members after 2025-07-01; PRO registers by 2026-07-01. Status: corrected 2026-10-27.
  • Simplified report 2026-05-31: Status: unverified (not in statute).
  • Needs assessment 2026-12-31; stewardship plan 2028-10-01. Status: verified 2026-10-27.
  • Reimbursement to service providers starts at 50% of net cost by 2029-02-01; PRO fee from 2029-01-01. Status: corrected 2026-10-27.
  • Source: https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/115A/full

Maryland

  • Exemption: under 1 ton or under $2M global gross revenue. Status: verified 2026-10-27. "Primary packaging only" qualifier: unverified.
  • PRO: CAA.
  • Registration date: unverified (secondary sources conflict: 2026-07-01 vs 2026-05-31; not in the statute, which leaves it to MDE regulation).
  • Plans due 2028-07-01. Status: verified 2026-10-27.
  • Sales ban: starts on a date set in MDE regulations; the draft's 2028-10-29 is unverified. Status: corrected 2026-10-27.
  • Source: https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/2025rs/Chapters_noln/CH_431_sb0901e.pdf

Washington

  • Exemption: de minimis volumes excluded, no numeric threshold found. Status: unverified.
  • PRO: CAA (announced 2026-03-04). Status: unverified.
  • Producers and PROs register with Ecology by 2026-03-01; producers in a PRO by 2026-07-01. Status: corrected 2026-10-27.
  • Ecology sets annual PRO fee by 2027-03-31 (draft's 2027-05-01 was an earlier House version). Status: corrected 2026-10-27.
  • Draft PRO plan 2028-10-01. Status: verified 2026-10-27.
  • Source (Final Bill Report, ch. 316 L 2025): https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/biennium/2025-26/Htm/Bill%20Reports/Senate/5284-S2.E%20SBR%20FBR%2025.htm

Maine

  • Exemptions (38 M.R.S. 2146(2)): under $2M prior-year revenue, except under $5M for the 3 years starting 1 year after the DEP-stewardship organization contract takes effect (draft had this reversed); under 1 ton of packaging; over 50% of revenue from salvage/closeout goods; first 15 tons of perishable food packaging. Status: corrected (revenue), verified (others) 2026-10-27.
  • DEP selects one stewardship organization by RFP for a 10-year term. Status: verified. Whether a contract exists yet: unverified. Registration expected around end of 2026: unverified.
  • Source: https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/38/title38sec2146.html
