Chasing subs for COIs, W-9s and lien waivers?

Sub Paperwork is a tool we plan to build so a small GC can see, in one list, which subs have sent a current COI, license, W-9 and lien waivers.

Early access: Sub Paperwork is not built yet. This page is a waitlist. Nothing is for sale.

We'll use your address only to email you about Sub Paperwork, for example when there's something to try. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Your address (and your answer, if you give one) goes to Buttondown, our email service. See the privacy policy.

The problem

If you're a small GC, every sub on a job owes you paperwork: a current certificate of insurance, a license, a W-9, lien waivers. Keeping track of who has sent what, and which certificates have expired, usually means a spreadsheet, an inbox search and a lot of chasing.

How it would work

This is a plan, not a product. Nothing below exists yet, and it may change.

  1. Add your subs.
  2. Record what each one has sent.
  3. See what's missing or expiring soon.

What we plan to build

One list of your subs

For each sub, whether you have a current COI, a license, a W-9 (received yes or no, and the date) and lien waivers.

Missing and expiring

A view of what's missing and what's expiring soon.

A link for each sub

A link you can send each sub so they can confirm their paperwork status.

Names, dates and status only

We don't plan to store tax ID numbers, bank details or copies of documents.
