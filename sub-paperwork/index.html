We decided not to build Sub Paperwork

We explored Sub Paperwork, a tracker for small general contractors to keep their subs' COIs, licenses, W-9s and lien waivers in one list. On 1 November 2026 we decided not to build it. It was never for sale.

If you joined the waitlist, we'll send you one email saying so. You can ask us to delete your address any time at support@smallfirelab.com.
