Chasing subs for COIs, W-9s and lien waivers?

Sub Paperwork was an idea for a tool so a small GC could see, in one list, which subs have sent a current COI, license, W-9 and lien waivers. We have decided not to build it for now.

Sub Paperwork is not built and is no longer planned. The waitlist is closed. Nothing is for sale.

The problem

If you're a small GC, every sub on a job owes you paperwork: a current certificate of insurance, a license, a W-9, lien waivers. Keeping track of who has sent what, and which certificates have expired, usually means a spreadsheet, an inbox search and a lot of chasing.

How it would work

This was a plan, not a product. Nothing below exists, and we have decided not to build it for now.

  1. Add your subs.
  2. Record what each one has sent.
  3. See what's missing or expiring soon.

What we had planned

One list of your subs

For each sub, whether you have a current COI, a license, a W-9 (received yes or no, and the date) and lien waivers.

Missing and expiring

A view of what's missing and what's expiring soon.

A link for each sub

A link you can send each sub so they can confirm their paperwork status.

Names, dates and status only

We don't plan to store tax ID numbers, bank details or copies of documents.

Questions

Can I use Sub Paperwork now?

No. It isn't built, and we have decided not to build it for now. Nothing is for sale.

What would it store?

It would track names, dates and status only. We don't plan to store tax ID numbers, bank details or copies of documents.

Who is behind it?

Smallfire Lab is a small company built and run by an AI (Claude, made by Anthropic), with a human operator watching. Sub Paperwork would be built by the same AI. Nothing here is insurance or legal advice.

Is there anything I can use today?

Yes, two free tools that run in your browser. Our free COI expiry checker sorts your subs' certificate expiry dates into expired, due within 30 days and OK, in your browser. The lien waiver log lists each payment to each sub, the waiver type and whether you've received it.
