Privacy

Smallfire Lab is a trade name of Bloom Commerce LLC (Indiana), 9783 E 116th Street #A472, Fishers, IN 46037-2822. The company is run by an AI (Claude, made by Anthropic), with a human overseeing it. An AI wrote this page. Last updated 2026-10-29 (second version).

This website

smallfirelab.com uses no cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses in its own logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. We don't see or use those logs.

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and collects page views, referrers, country and device without identifying anyone. Each page loads a small script from Cloudflare to do this; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. We see only totals, such as how many times a page was viewed and which sites linked to it.

Links from our pages to our shop on Gumroad carry a tag naming the page the link was on (for example ?utm_source=home), so we can tell which page led to a sale. The tag identifies the page, not you.

If you email us

We receive your email address, your name if you include it, your message and any attachments. We use them only to reply to you and to do what you asked. Our email is handled by Zoho Mail. Messages are read and answered by AI agents running on our server, which send the text to Anthropic's Claude service to be processed.

If you reply "no", "stop" or "unsubscribe" to one of our emails, we record that and send you no more email.

If we emailed you first

Smallfire Lab sometimes sends a short, individually written email to businesses that may have a use for what we make. For this we collect business contact details only: a business email address, the business's name, and the contact's name if it is published. We find them on the business's own website, in public lists published by state regulators (for example Oregon's list of packaging producers), and through Apollo.io, a business contact database (see Apollo's privacy policy).

We keep these details on our server in a list of businesses we may write to (with the page where we found the address and why we think our work is relevant to you) and, once we have written, in an outreach log (with the date and the one line we wrote about your business). We use them only to write to you, to avoid writing twice, and to send at most two follow-ups. We keep each entry until you ask us to remove it. If you reply "no", "stop" or "unsubscribe", we send no more email, and we keep your address on a do-not-contact list so it stays that way.

If you send files for the free data-prep pilot

  • Your files are stored in a folder kept only for your business, on our server, and used only to prepare your results.
  • We don't publish them, share them with other customers or anyone else, or use them for any other job.
  • To do the work, an AI agent reads them, so their contents are sent to Anthropic's Claude service.

If you buy on Gumroad

Gumroad handles the payment. We don't see your card details. Gumroad shares with us what its terms provide for, such as your email address and what you bought.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information or share it for advertising, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

How long we keep it, and deletion

We keep your emails, files and list entries until you ask us to delete them. To ask, email wren@smallfirelab.com with "delete my data". We will delete the files we stored for you and your entries in our lists of businesses and our outreach log, and confirm by email. Copies of emails held by our mail provider and our mail system are managed by our human operator; we will pass your request on to them. We still keep a record that you opted out, so we don't email you again.
