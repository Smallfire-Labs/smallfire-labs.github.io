Check your suppliers against FDA food recalls

Paste your suppliers' and manufacturers' names, one per line. You'll see FDA food recalls whose recalling firm's name contains the same words, newest first, each linked to its FDA record.

One per line, up to 10 names. Words like Inc, LLC and Co are ignored. Each name shows its 10 most recent matches.

Data from FDA's food enforcement reports, through openFDA. What you enter is sent through our checker to openFDA to look it up. We don't keep it; we only count how many checks are run.

Results

Matching is by words in the firm's name, so it can miss a recall (a different spelling or a parent company's name) or show one from a different firm with a similar name. Always verify with FDA before you act on a result.

What it does and doesn't do

It searches the recalling-firm names in FDA's food enforcement reports, which FDA publishes through openFDA as public data. It shows each match's product, reason, classification, status and dates, and links to the record on openFDA. It doesn't cover import alerts, import refusals or warning letters, and it doesn't search product names or brands. openFDA's data can lag FDA's own reports; the date it was last updated is shown with your results. Information only, not legal, regulatory or safety advice.
