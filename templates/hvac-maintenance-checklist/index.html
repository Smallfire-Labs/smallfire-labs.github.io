The checklist

Do the spring list when you service the cooling system and the fall list for the heating system. Skip what doesn't apply to the equipment and mark it N/A.

Spring: cooling system (10 items)

  1. Thermostat: settings and schedule suit the customer when home and away
  2. Electrical: connections inspected for tightness; motor voltage and current measured and recorded
  3. Moving parts: lubricated where the manufacturer calls for it
  4. Condensate drain: inspected and clear; pan free of standing water
  5. Controls: starting cycle checked; unit starts, runs and shuts off properly
  6. Indoor (evaporator) and outdoor (condenser) coils: inspected and cleaned as needed
  7. Refrigerant level: checked by a certified technician and result recorded (record only; this sheet gives no handling steps)
  8. Blower: cleaned and adjusted; airflow looks right at the registers
  9. Air filter: checked; replaced or customer shown how to change it
  10. Outdoor unit: area around it clear of leaves, weeds and debris

Fall: heating system (10 items)

  1. Thermostat: settings and schedule suit the customer when home and away
  2. Electrical: connections inspected for tightness; motor voltage and current measured and recorded
  3. Moving parts: lubricated where the manufacturer calls for it
  4. Condensate drain (high-efficiency furnace or heat pump in cooling mode): inspected and clear
  5. Controls: starting cycle checked; unit starts, runs and shuts off properly
  6. Gas or oil connections, gas pressure, burner combustion and heat exchanger: inspected by a qualified technician; result recorded
  7. Blower: cleaned and adjusted; airflow looks right at the registers
  8. Air filter: checked; replaced or customer shown how to change it
  9. Flue / vent and surrounding area: visually checked for obstruction or damage; result recorded
  10. Carbon monoxide alarm present and tested by the customer (note only)

Based on the check-up items published by ENERGY STAR, with two added items (a flue/vent check and a carbon monoxide alarm note) and some items reworded.
