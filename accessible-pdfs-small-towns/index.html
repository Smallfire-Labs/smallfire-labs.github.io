Which town PDFs must be accessible by April 26, 2028, and which are exempt

Under 50,000 people, or a special district: April 26, 2028.

If your town, county or township has fewer than 50,000 people, or you're a special district (water, fire, library and similar), the Justice Department's web rule applies to you from April 26, 2028. From then on, the PDFs you post must meet WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA. PDFs already posted before that date are exempt, unless people still use them to apply for or take part in your services.

The date moved. The rule of April 24, 2024 set April 26, 2027 for smaller entities. An interim final rule, effective April 20, 2026, extended it to April 26, 2028 (Federal Register 2026-07663, 91 FR 20902). Entities of 50,000 or more moved from April 24, 2026 to April 26, 2027. Some pages still show the old dates.

This page quotes the rule (28 CFR part 35, subpart H, as published on eCFR, checked November 10, 2026). It isn't legal advice; ask your attorney how it applies to you.

"Total population" is the Census Bureau's figure for your entity in the most recent decennial Census (§ 35.104). Independent school districts use the Census Bureau's Small Area Income and Poverty Estimates instead.

Are PDFs covered?

Yes, and so are word processor, presentation and spreadsheet files. The rule calls them "conventional electronic documents": "web content or content in mobile apps that is in the following electronic file formats: portable document formats (“PDF”), word processor file formats, presentation file formats, and spreadsheet file formats" (§ 35.104). It also covers content you provide "directly or through contractual, licensing, or other arrangements" (§ 35.200), so PDFs that your agenda software or another vendor posts for you count too.

The archive test: all four must be true

Under § 35.104, archived web content is content that:

  1. Was created before your compliance date, or reproduces paper documents or other physical media created before it;
  2. Is kept only for reference, research or recordkeeping;
  3. Isn't changed or updated after it's archived; and
  4. Is stored in a dedicated area "clearly identified as being archived".

A folder of old minutes mixed in with current documents doesn't meet condition 4. A separate page or section labelled "Archive" can.

If it would cost too much

§ 35.204 lets an entity show that full compliance would cause "undue financial and administrative burdens". The decision must be made by the head of the entity, or someone they designate, after considering all resources available, and it must be "accompanied by a written statement of the reasons". The entity still has to comply as far as it can without that burden.

Questions

When must a town under 50,000 make its PDFs accessible?

From April 26, 2028, under 28 CFR 35.200(b)(2). The date was April 26, 2027 until an interim final rule, effective April 20, 2026, extended it (Federal Register 2026-07663).

Do we have to fix every old PDF on our website?

No. PDFs posted before your compliance date are exempt under 28 CFR 35.201(b), unless they are currently used to apply for, gain access to, or participate in your services, programs or activities. Application forms still in use must be made accessible.

What counts as an archive under the rule?

Content created before your compliance date, kept only for reference, research or recordkeeping, not changed after archiving, and stored in an area clearly identified as archived. All four conditions must hold (28 CFR 35.104).

Is a water or fire district a special district?

It is if it meets the definition in 28 CFR 35.104: a public entity, other than a county, municipality, township or independent school district, authorized by state law to provide one function or a limited number of designated functions, with enough administrative and fiscal autonomy to qualify as a separate government, and whose population the Census Bureau does not calculate. Every special district government has the April 26, 2028 date, whatever its size.

What standard do the PDFs have to meet?

WCAG 2.1 Level A and Level AA success criteria and conformance requirements (28 CFR 35.200(b)).
