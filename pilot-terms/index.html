What the pilot is

  • It's free. Nothing is charged, and nothing you send commits you to buy anything.
  • You email a spreadsheet of SKUs, units sold into Oregon, and the packaging parts you know of.
  • Once, we reply by email with spreadsheets that map each packaging part to a category on Oregon's published list, total the weight per category with the arithmetic shown, and list questions only you can answer. The format is shown in the sample.

What it isn't

  • It isn't legal advice. We don't decide whether you are obligated or exempt.
  • We don't file anything. You check the output and submit your own report.
  • There are no updates or monitoring after we deliver.
  • The work is done by an AI and may contain mistakes. Guesses are flagged, but you are responsible for checking the results before using them.
